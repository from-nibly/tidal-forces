# v0.29.0 — Guarded prepared-next playback

Approved after native preview review.

- Fixes a v0.28 PCM span bug: different source/output sample rates could restart
  Rodio's resampler every frame and produce the wrong playback duration. Constant
  format spans now match reference rate/channel conversion sample-for-sample.
- Prepares one known queued successor during the last 30 seconds of playback,
  with at most two speculative attempts per playback generation.
  Compatible FLAC sources with equal decoded rates/channels can continue through
  the same output source and sink, without a stop/recreate or stream request at
  the boundary. No crossfade or added DSP.
- Queue edits race an atomic cancel/claim boundary. If playback already advanced,
  the UI reconciles that occurrence before applying the edit. Account, revision,
  requested quality and unique playback generations guard promotion and seeking.
- Final listening credit belongs to the previous track; next-track progress starts
  at its boundary. Pauses and synthesized underrun silence do not count.
- The promoted song retains its temporary compressed buffer for local seeks.
  Existing per-stream 256 MiB storage and PCM/preparation bounds remain; there is
  at most one prepared successor, not an album download or offline library.

## Limits

Continuous joining is currently limited to compatible FLAC sources. AAC
priming/padding, rate/channel changes, unprepared successors and context-repeat
wrap/shuffle use ordinary transitions. Preparation failures do not interrupt the
current song or auto-skip the next one. Late data or handoff contention can still
require the ordinary fallback. Source labels do not certify DAC output or bit-perfect
hardware playback. Output-device recovery, DSP, unavailable-song styling/skipping
and P5 radio continuation remain separate work.

## Verification

360 normal tests pass; the normal suite excludes five opt-in tests. Formatting,
strict Clippy on Rust 1.95 and 1.99, release checking and the development build pass.
The two separately invoked isolated D-Bus contracts and private XDG/D-Bus/Xvfb,
software-GL/null-ALSA native fixtures/smoke also pass.

Synthetic tests compare exact PCM joins and output conversion, exercise claim/cancel
races, pause/underflow accounting, stale queue/account/seek guards, duplicate and
repeat occurrences, history attribution, and drag revisions. FLAC preparation,
joining and promoted-song seeking also run after fixture HTTP servers close.
No agent-driven real-account or audible/device tests are authorized.
