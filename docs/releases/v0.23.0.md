# v0.23.0 — Guarded owned-playlist deletion

- Group Edit details and Delete playlist under the owned playlist header’s **Playlist…** menu. Deleting the entire playlist requires typing **DELETE** and explicitly clicking the destructive button; this is not unfollowing or removing only filtered/loaded tracks.
- Freeze account, playlist and revision. Recheck ownership/revision before one conditional DELETE, then require a missing response from that exact playlist resource after acknowledgment. Permission, transport, refresh and rate-limit errors are not absence.
- Cancel queued work when navigating, refreshing, changing accounts or closing. Uncertain outcomes require Refresh and inspection; never blindly retry or roll back.
- Invalidate stale browse data and return to the library after verified deletion. Queue entries and local pins are kept; further loading from the deleted playlist may fail.
- Guard request identity across token renewal and redact missing-resource identities from displayed/debug errors.

Verified with 260 normal tests, strict Clippy/format/release checks, isolated instance/Secret Service contracts, native responsive confirmation fixtures and signed-out smoke checks. Tests include stale/foreign scope, cancellation around the write, exact absence versus denial, backpressure, actual confirmation-button clicks and queue preservation. No agent-run live account deletions or audible/device tests; synthetic contracts do not prove live deletion or conditional-header interoperability.
